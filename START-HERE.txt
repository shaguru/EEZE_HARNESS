RustPortableHarness - quick start for students
==============================================

1. Unzip first. Right-click the ZIP file > "Extract All..." > choose your Desktop > Extract.
   Do NOT double-click the app while it is still inside the ZIP.

2. Open the new RustPortableHarness folder and double-click RustPortableHarness.exe.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", only click "More info" > "Run anyway"
   if your school allows it. Otherwise ask your teacher.

3. At the top, pick the AI you have an account for:
      Codex  = uses a ChatGPT account
      Claude = uses a Claude account

4. Click "+ New project", type a name and pick a type (for example "Python window app").
   The app makes the folder, its Python or Rust environment and starter code.
   (Or click "Open existing folder" for a project you already have.)

5. Click "Sign in". Your web browser opens. Sign in with YOUR OWN account, then come back.

6. Type your question in the message box and press Enter.
   Press "Run" to start your program, or "Terminal" for a console where
   python, cargo, git, node and npx work.
   The AI asks before it changes files or runs commands. Read each request, then
   click "Approve once" or "Decline".

Handy tips
----------
- Move the message box: drag its dotted handle, or click the small left / bottom / right
  buttons on the message box.
- See how much you have used: the 5h (five hours) and Wk (weekly) bars at the top.
  Click them to see all your accounts.
- Something wrong? The app shows a short explanation and a button that helps.
  Teachers: Settings > Advanced has network, certificate and diagnostics options.

Before you leave a shared computer
----------------------------------
Click the bars at the top > "Sign out". Do not copy the "data" folder to other people:
it contains your sign-in.
