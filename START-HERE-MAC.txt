RustPortableHarness for Mac - quick start
=========================================

1. Double-click the downloaded ZIP. A folder named RustPortableHarness appears.
   Move it to your Desktop or Documents.

2. macOS blocks apps from the internet that are not from the App Store or signed by Apple.
   This app is free and unsigned, so do this ONCE:
     - Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities > Terminal).
     - Type:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine
       then a space, then drag the RustPortableHarness FOLDER into the Terminal window,
       and press Return.
   (Or: double-click the app, click Done, then open System Settings > Privacy & Security
    and click "Open Anyway". You may need to do this for codex too.)

3. Double-click RustPortableHarness.app.

4. Pick Codex (ChatGPT account) or Claude (Claude account) at the top, click "+ New project"
   or "Open existing folder", click "Sign in" with YOUR OWN account, and type your question.

Notes for Mac
-------------
- Settings, sign-ins and chats are saved in the "data" folder next to the app. Keep the app
  and the data folder together.
- Programming tools (Python, Rust, Git, PowerShell, Node.js) are downloaded by the app on
  Windows only for now. On a Mac, install the ones you need yourself; the app finds them.
  Dictation (the microphone button) works on Mac: get "Dictation (Parakeet)" in
  Programming tools, then allow the microphone when macOS asks.
- Claude: install Claude Code for your Mac (https://claude.com/claude-code); the app finds it.
